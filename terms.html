Alpha — please read before using

Good Beer Here is an early, in-progress test version. This isn't formal legal terms — just a plain-language heads-up.

What this is

This is an alpha test build, shared with a small group of people to try out. Things may break, change, or be reset without notice while it's being developed.

Location data

When you submit a report, your device's GPS location at that moment is captured and stored, to confirm you were actually near the venue. No account, name, or other personal details are collected — reporting is fully anonymous.

Accuracy

Venue information and reports are user-submitted and not independently verified beyond the checks built into the app. Don't rely on it as your only source before travelling somewhere specifically for it.

Use at your own risk

This is an unfinished, unsupported test project. There's no guarantee of uptime, accuracy, or that data will be preserved — please don't rely on it for anything important.

Questions

If something looks broken or you're unsure about anything, just ask whoever shared this with you.
